About iChartWrightAI

Built by people who sold Wall Street's answers
and wanted a more honest one.

iChartWrightAI exists because disciplined, rules-based market signals shouldn't require a private-bank relationship — and shouldn't come wrapped in hype, tips, or someone else's hands on your account.

The premise is simple. Most market content fails in one of two ways: it's personalized advice dressed up as research, or it's an indicator firehose with no conviction behind it. iChartWrightAI is neither. One methodology is designed to publish one verdict per instrument, per timeframe — the same content, to every subscriber, at the same time. It will never be tailored, never be account-aware, and never place a trade.

The discipline is the product. A fixed 46-instrument universe. Daily plus six intraday timeframes. Flat at every close. A shipment bar the methodology must clear before results are ever claimed — not a number tuned to look good in a backtest. Anything we publish in the future will be published only if the rules allow it.
