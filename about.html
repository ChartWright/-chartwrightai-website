About ChartWrightAI

Built by people who sold Wall Street's answers
and wanted a more honest one.

ChartWrightAI exists because disciplined, rules-based market signals shouldn't require a private-bank relationship — and shouldn't come wrapped in hype, tips, or someone else's hands on your account.

The premise is simple. Most market content fails in one of two ways: it's personalized advice dressed up as research, or it's an indicator firehose with no conviction behind it. ChartWrightAI is neither. One methodology publishes one verdict per instrument, per timeframe — the same content, to every subscriber, at the same time. It is never tailored, never account-aware, and never places a trade.

The discipline is the product. A fixed 46-instrument universe. Daily plus six intraday timeframes. Flat at every close. A shipment bar the methodology must clear before results are ever claimed — not a number tuned to look good in a backtest. What we publish, we publish because the rules said so.
