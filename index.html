Questions, answered straight

The answers below are the product. If an answer ever changes, the product changed.

What is available today?

This website is a development preview and research-list signup. Paid subscriptions and live signal delivery are not open. Product structure, disclosures, and launch language remain under validation and legal review.

Do you ever touch my account?

Never. ChartWrightAI places no orders, holds no custody, has no discretion, and never reads your balances or positions. If a tool you use connects to your broker, that's your tool acting under your control — not ours.

Why is signal content identical on every tier?

Because that's what keeps the signals impersonal and auditable. No tier gets earlier, deeper, or different signal content. Tiers will differ only in delivery and service.

How are signals delivered?

Structured for machine consumption, so your own agents and workflows can read them directly. Final delivery formats are being finalized and will be documented before launch.

Will the signals make me money?

We don't know — and anyone who answers "yes" is selling you something. Markets carry real risk, and no methodology wins every trade. What we promise is narrower and checkable: rules-based signals, published identically to everyone, from a methodology whose discipline we hold ourselves to in public. What you do with them — and how you manage risk — stays in your hands.

What does "EOD flat" mean?

The methodology closes every session flat — no published signal ever carries a position overnight.

Why only 46 instruments?

A fixed, liquid, U.S.-listed universe — the Dow 30, the 11 SPDR sector ETFs, and 5 market anchors — keeps the methodology testable and the signal set honest. No ticker-chasing. ChartWrightAI Research covers a wider set — including major crypto and fixed-income ETFs — but coverage is charting and analysis, never a signal.
