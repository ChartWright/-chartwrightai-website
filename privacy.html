What we collect

When you sign up for our newsletter and research, we collect your email address. That is the only personal information this site collects. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not collect account, brokerage, balance, or position information — ever, by design.

How we use it

We do not sell your email address, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

Signup emails are stored in ChartWrightAI's own Google Workspace account. Google acts as our infrastructure provider and processes this data under its own terms.

Your choices

Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. You can also email jack@ichartwrightai.com or dan@ichartwrightai.com at any time to be removed or to ask what we hold about you — the answer will be your email address or nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and material changes will be announced to the list before they take effect.
